=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 132 characters) ===

Highlight local .md and .html files and the web. Your marks come back — and never leave your device.


=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===

Mark up what you read the way you would mark up paper.

Select text, pick a colour or an underline, and it stays. Close the tab, restart the
browser, come back a week later — the marks are still there.


WORKS ON YOUR OWN FILES, NOT JUST THE WEB

Most highlighters only reach web pages. This one also works on documents opened
straight from your disk: local .md and .html files, exported documentation, README
files, API references, runbooks and specs.

That is the reason it exists. If you read technical documentation offline, nothing else
covers it well.


NOTHING LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

No account. No sign-in. No server. No network request anywhere in the extension.

Your highlights are stored by the extension on your own machine. They are not in a
cookie, so they are never sent to a website. They are not in the page's own storage, so
a site cannot read them or wipe them. There is no cloud, no sync, and nothing to leak.


SIX COLOURS, PLUS UNDERLINE — AND THEY COMBINE

Yellow, green, pink, blue, orange, purple, and a separate underline. A passage can be
yellow AND underlined; pressing one never clears the other. Press the same thing again
to remove it. One button clears every highlight on the page, with a confirmation step.

The palette was chosen by measurement, not by eye: every colour keeps text at WCAG AAA
contrast, and the colours are kept apart in brightness so they stay distinguishable
under colour vision deficiency.


FIND YOUR WAY AROUND A LONG DOCUMENT

A panel docks to either edge of the page, and folds away when you do not want it.

One tab is the document outline — every heading, indented by level, click to jump.
This works on local Markdown files too, where there are no headings for an extension
to read: the outline is built from the "# " syntax in the text itself.

The other tab lists every highlight on the page, in the order they appear in the
document. Click one and the page scrolls to it and frames it for a moment, so you never
lose a mark in a long file again.

The panel remembers which side you put it on, and whether you prefer light or dark.


WRITE A NOTE ON A PASSAGE

Select a sentence, press the note button, and write. A small dot appears at the end of
the passage; click it and your note opens exactly where you left it.

Notes are stored with the mark they belong to, so when the document is edited and the
passage moves, the note travels with it. They are listed in the panel as well, on the
row of the highlight they belong to.


IT DOES NOT BREAK THE PAGE

Highlighting is drawn with the CSS Custom Highlight API rather than by wrapping your
text in extra elements. Page layout is not disturbed, selections that span headings,
lists and tables work correctly, and copied text stays clean.


MARKS SURVIVE THE DOCUMENT CHANGING

Each highlight remembers the exact text plus the words around it. When a document is
edited and the passage moves, the mark is found again by matching that context. If the
text is deleted, the highlight is not thrown away — it simply waits, and returns if the
text comes back.

Renaming or moving a local file does not lose your marks either.


ALSO WORKS INSIDE EMBEDDED FRAMES

Some pages render their real content in an embedded frame. Those are supported. Frames
with almost no text are skipped, so ad and tracking frames cost nothing.


PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Installing asks for nothing about websites. Access is granted by you, when you want it:

• Local files — Chrome keeps this behind its own switch. Open the extension's details
  page and turn on "Allow access to file URLs". The extension cannot do this for you;
  only you can.

• Websites — open the toolbar popup and choose "Enable on this site" or "Enable on all
  sites". You can turn it off again the same way, at any time.

The extension reads the address of the tab you are on only to tell you whether
highlighting is available there.


OPEN SOURCE

REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITS

• Chrome 105 or newer
• Chrome does not let any extension work on chrome:// pages, the Web Store, or other
  extensions' pages
• Tags and search do not exist yet
• Some single-page applications rewrite their address after loading, which can make a
  mark harder to find again
